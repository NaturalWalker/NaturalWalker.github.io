I'm a defensive security researcher focused on malware analysis and cyber threat
intelligence. This site keeps two things deliberately separate:

- **Blog** — reverse-engineering notes, CTI on tracked actors and infrastructure,
  and detection engineering (YARA, Sigma, hunting queries).
- **Writeups** — CTF challenge solutions across reversing, forensics, and more.

Everything here is for defenders. Malware is analysed in isolated environments;
samples and live infrastructure are never linked directly, and indicators are
shared in defanged form.
